## Narration, read straight through

There's a hole in every AI project. It eats your progress while you sleep.

It's called context rot. And if you've built anything with AI, you've met it.

Here's where it comes from. Every AI session starts with an empty memory. You explain your project, and it fills up. The session ends, and all of it is gone. Even inside one long session, the window fills, and the oldest things fall out first. Notes and memory files help, until they go stale, because somebody has to keep them current.

So you re-explain. The agent rebuilds things that already exist. Two agents change the same file, and nobody notices. Hours every week, pulled into the hole.

The fix is simple to say. Keep what's true about your project outside the chat, somewhere every session and every agent reads first. The hole can't eat what it can't reach.

If context rot has eaten your work before, check us out. We solved it.
