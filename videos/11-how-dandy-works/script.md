## Narration, read straight through

How does Dandy work?

You connect Dandy to your AI coding tool, once.

Describe what you're building, and your agent lays it out as a map:
every piece, and what each one needs from the others.

Before any work, it reads the map.
As it builds, it marks each piece done, and checks it.

Change one piece, and Dandy flags everything connected to it.

So the map is always current, without you keeping it that way.

## Accuracy notes

- "Connect once": Dandy connects to the coding tool through a connector; setup is one time. Whether to name
  Claude Code here is Charlie's call (10 and 12 do not name it).
- "Marks each piece done, and checks it": statuses plus the review step each piece passes before it counts as done.
- "Flags everything connected": a change to one piece's shape flows along its connections to the pieces that use it.
