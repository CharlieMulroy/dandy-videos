## Narration, read straight through

Why use Dandy?

Because you stop re-explaining your project every time you open a new chat.

Because you can leave a project for weeks, and pick it up in a minute.

Because when one piece changes, you see what it touches before it breaks.

And because you can finally see where every project stands.

Dandy keeps your projects moving, so you finish what you start.

## Accuracy notes

- Four benefits, each something the product does today: the agent reads the project's state at the start of a
  session; the state persists between sessions; a change to a piece flows to the pieces connected to it; each
  project shows the status of its pieces.
- "Pick it up in a minute" is a figure of speech, not a measured time. Softer option: "and pick it right back up".
- "Finish what you start" is the promise of the series; it is an aim, not a guarantee, and reads that way.
- No claim about tokens, speed or cost.

## Messaging check

- Does not call Dandy a project management tool, a harness or a framework; no "state layer"; no long dashes.
- Dandy Man appears because this is an informational video about Dandy; end card with him.

## Decisions

1. "Pick it up in a minute" stays (Charlie read it that way, Nov 19).
2. The three example projects stay as Booking app, Portfolio, Budget tool.
