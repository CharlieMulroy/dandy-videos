## Narration, read straight through

Why use Dandy?

Because you stop re-explaining your project every time you open a new chat.

Because you can leave a project for weeks, and pick it up in a minute.

Because when one piece changes, you see what it touches before it breaks.

And because you can finally see where every project stands.

Dandy keeps your projects moving, so you finish what you start.

## Accuracy notes

- Four benefits, each one something the product does today. No claim about tokens, speed or cost.
- "Pick it up in a minute" is a figure of speech, not a measured time; soften to "pick it right back up" if preferred.
