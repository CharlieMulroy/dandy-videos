## Narration, as recorded

Okay, follow along while I explain our product using a Thanksgiving metaphor. My cofounder did not approve this, but I'm gonna do it anyway. So, the year 1621. The pilgrims are making their first ever Thanksgiving dinner. The feast represents your project. The pilgrims are your AI agents. They're hard workers. Extremely fast. However, one problem is that every morning they wake up and forget everything. Who's catching the turkey? Who's on corn? What's already cooking? So you end up with three pots of corn, you have no turkey, and somebody moved the feast up a day, and nobody told the guy with the pie. Dandy's the scroll that's nailed to the wall. It holds every dish, who's on it, what it needs, what's already done. Before they cook anything, every pilgrim checks it. And after any task they finish, they update it. So if the feast gets moved again, they are all aware. And if you run out of any ingredient, every dish that depends on it gets flagged. It's the same pilgrims, but the feast actually happens. Is this metaphor perfect? No. But Happy Thanksgiving. Go try Dandy.
